# Independent software

YTM Listen is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google, YouTube,
Discord, Cloudflare, or the artists whose music you play. Trademarks belong to
their respective owners. The app uses original artwork for its icon.

The music player is the actual YouTube Music website. This project does not
download, redistribute, proxy, or decrypt audio; remove ads; bypass access
controls; or grant Premium access. Each participant streams independently
through their own account, with their own subscriptions and regional access.
This architecture does not guarantee compliance with any service's terms;
users are responsible for their use of those services.

Google may reject embedded browser sign-in, and website changes can break the
bridge. A desktop WebView is not a supported Google browser merely because it
uses a Chromium engine. No sign-in or Premium playback support is guaranteed.
Do not disable browser security, copy authentication cookies from another
browser, or share credentials as a workaround.

Room codes are invitations, not passwords. Anyone with a code can join while
the room exists. The host role is protected separately by a random reconnect
credential held in memory. Use a trusted relay; its operator can see the
current video ID, playback position, display names, and network addresses.
Incognito hides Discord presence only; it does not hide activity in a room.
